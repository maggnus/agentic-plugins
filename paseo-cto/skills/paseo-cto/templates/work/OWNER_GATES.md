# Owner gates

Decisions the CTO may not take. Product direction, pricing, legal exposure, external commitments,
publication, live mutation, money, and irreversible operations belong here rather than in a plan
filled with a plausible guess.

Each open gate either blocks a named work unit — which then carries `state: blocked` and this gate as
its blocker — or is recorded here before any work depends on it.

| Gate | Decision required | Blocks | Opened | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `<G1>` | `<the exact question the owner must answer>` | `<W1-AB-01b>` | `<dd/mm>` | `<answer and date, once given>` |

A resolved gate keeps its row with the recorded answer, so a later reader sees what was decided and
when.
