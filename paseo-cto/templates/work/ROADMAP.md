# Roadmap

What the product must become, in milestones a person can check. [BOARD.md](BOARD.md) holds every
task and its state, grouped by these milestones. One milestone is open at a time; it closes on its
proving journey, not on a share of tasks.

| M1 | <observable state> | <the steps a stranger takes to see it> |

## Admission rule for ideas and defects

An idea becomes a task only when it blocks the open milestone's exit, a proving journey or a
critical check reproduces the absence, and the change is the smallest reviewable way to remove it.
Otherwise it goes to [FINDINGS.md](FINDINGS.md) with a return trigger.
