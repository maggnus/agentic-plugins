Derive section types, rhythm, and grid proportions from the subject's design spec, apply an economical palette (neutral base + one restrained focal `--accent`, adding extra hues only for distinct data or real alerts, and expressing hierarchy through whitespace, typography, and hairline rules rather than dark or colored blocks alone), vary section structures (tables, interactive tools, asymmetric splits) instead of repeating uniform 3-card grids, and keep every visual value bound to the `:root` theme.
